# ADR-004: Afyanex as founding design partner and proof site
**Status:** Accepted (2026-08-16)
**Context:** Founder owns Afyanex; its front-desk staff are available daily; live patient traffic exists.
**Decision:** Afyanex is tenant #1 from Phase 1 seed onward; its front-desk staff are the primary persona for inbox design; go-live acceptance (TESTING.md) is run there; before/after metrics form the first case study.
**Consequences:** Risk of over-fitting to one clinic — mitigated by onboarding 5 external design partners in weeks 7–10 before self-serve. Afyanex-specific configuration must live in seed/config, never in code.
